About Us

Al-Khizana was created by Nabil EL MAALMI, a student at École Supérieure de l'Éducation et de la Formation (ESEF) Berrechid, to bring Primary Education course materials together in one organized place — instead of leaving students to track lessons down one by one. The initiative is independent, entirely free, and not affiliated with the school's administration.

How the archive grows

The archive fills in gradually, module by module, rather than launching full and staying that way. Here's the process we're following:

Collecting lessons

Materials are gathered directly from students, module by module, semester by semester — starting with Primary Education.

Sorting and tagging

Each file is tagged by year, semester, module, and type — lesson, summary, past exam, or exercises — before it's published here.

Growing in stages

Once Primary Education is complete, the archive opens to other specializations under the same standards.
